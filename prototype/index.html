    How the numbers work

    Ranges, not points

    • Every result shows low, central and high.
    • Tax levers use HMRC's static costings; behaviour is not modelled in v0.
    • GDP effects use spending multipliers of 0.4–1.0 and tax multipliers of 0.2–0.6.

    Every number has a label

    • sourced taken from an official release, linked.
    • approx derived or scaled; method shown.
    • training placeholder until the data pipeline replaces it.
    • plug a residual that makes the statement balance; must be broken down before launch.
